# Santa Maria

La Santa María de la Inmaculada Concepción or La Santa Maria, originally La Gallega, The Galician, was the largest of the three small ships sailed by Christopher Columbus in his first expedition across the Atlantic Ocean in 1492, with the backing of the Catholic Monarchs of Spain.

Sailwind has a notable lack of carrack and galleon style ships, and this mod aims to fix this. Currently only the Santa Maria (Carrack) is implemented, there will be more coming. 

The carrack is located at Siren Song next to the cog, and is bought for 80 gold.

## Dependencies
- BepInEx 5

## Installation
[Download](https://github.com/winterspices/AgeOfExploration/releases/latest) the latest release, and unzip the contents into your `Plugins` folder. It should look like this:
```
plugins\
  AgeOfExploration\
    exploration
    exploration.dll
    exploration.manifest
```
